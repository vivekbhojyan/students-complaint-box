About ABES Engineering College

ABES Engineering College is one of the premier engineering institutions in Uttar Pradesh, committed to academic excellence, innovation, and holistic development of students. The college provides a strong foundation in technical education combined with ethical values and professional skills.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and an industry-oriented curriculum, ABES focuses on preparing students to meet real-world challenges and contribute meaningfully to society.

About the Complaint Portal

The Student Complaint Portal is an initiative to provide a transparent, secure, and user-friendly platform for students to raise their concerns related to academics, infrastructure, internet facilities, examinations, and other campus services.

Our goal is to ensure that every student's voice is heard and addressed in a timely and responsible manner, thereby improving the overall campus experience.

Our Objectives

  • Provide a safe and transparent grievance redressal system
  • Improve communication between students and administration
  • Ensure timely resolution of student issues
  • Enhance the quality of campus life
